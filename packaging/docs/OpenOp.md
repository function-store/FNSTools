## Why use it

Some operators matter and sit six levels deep: the Level TOP that sets the
brightness of the whole output, the Constant CHOP with the tempo. Getting to
them means clicking down the path bar every time you want to check or nudge
them.

OpenOp keeps a reference to them where you can reach it. Place one beside a
control panel, or several in a small dashboard, and the important nodes are one
click away.

## How to use it

1. Pick OpenOp from the **FNS** tab of the OP Create dialog. It lands as one
   node; put it wherever you will see it.
2. Drag the operator you want onto its **OP** field. For several operators, drop
   them all, or type their paths separated by spaces.
3. Switch on **Par Window**, **Viewer Window** or both, for the windows you
   want.
4. Pulse **Open**. The windows come up from wherever you are in the network.
