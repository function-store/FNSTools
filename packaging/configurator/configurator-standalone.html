  SINGLE DOWNLOAD
  
Downloading one tool on its own

  
You can: drop the .tox into any project and the tool works by itself.
    The intended way is an install, though, because a copy downloaded here
    never updates itself.

  
To get fixes as they ship, tick the tools you want on
    this page and press Copy install script, or
    download FNSTools.tox
    and pick your tools inside TouchDesigner.
